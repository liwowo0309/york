# Keep the day 6 save

## Change

The new game now looks through every copy of the save, can import and export, and keeps day 6 dad/mom jobs the same. The usual play address is `http://127.0.0.1:8777`.

## Rationale

Progress is stored per address. Opening `localhost:8080` looks like a new game even when day 6 is still on 8777.

## What we refused

- We did not invent a new save key. The old key still holds the live progress.
- We did not replace a richer save with a reconstructed one automatically.
