# Wardrobe, books, and a readable bag

## Change

Clothes stay in a home wardrobe, so a new skirt no longer hides the school uniform. The clothes shop also sells hats, hair flowers, and shoes. A street bookstore and a home desk raise a new learning stat; harder books wait until that number is high enough. The bag uses icons, and the fridge asks whether to put food in or eat.

## Rationale

Wearing one outfit used to feel like losing the others. Food in the bag and food in the fridge were the same number, so putting apples away looked like eating them.

## What we refused

- Buying still does not auto-wear. New clothes go to the wardrobe.
- The bookstore is one place to buy and read. Home has its own desk.
