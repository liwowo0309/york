# Real classmates and class on arrival

## Change

依月自己的教室里坐着真正的同班同学：如意、然然、杨鹏一、伊丽娜、杨乐彤。一走进教室，老师立刻开课，不再只限早上。

## Rationale

The classroom should feel like her real class. Waiting until morning to sit down before class made school feel closed after the first outing.

## What we refused

- Classmates stay with her grade as she levels up; other floors still have their own kids.
- Coming back from the bathroom does not start another class by itself.
