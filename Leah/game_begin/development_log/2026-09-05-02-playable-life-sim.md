# First playable life sim

## Change

A single-file browser prototype: `index.html`, `style.css`, and a large `game.js` IIFE covering home, street, shops, park, school, and help-for-gold.

## Rationale

Ship a walkable world before splitting files. Canvas 2D plus `localStorage` keeps the game offline and install-free.

## What we refused

- No engine, bundler, or accounts yet. Those add cost before the loop is fun.
- No combat. The loop is helping, shopping, school, and family.
