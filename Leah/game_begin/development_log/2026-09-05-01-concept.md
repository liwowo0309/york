# Concept: a house, a family, a street

## Context

The first design came from a child’s description of everyday life, kept in `wobble.md`.

## What it asked for

- Four people at home: dad, mom, brother, and 依月 (Leah).
- Home actions: turtles, coffee, sleep, water, fridge.
- A street with shops, a car, food, clothes, seeds, bird cages.
- Money only after helping other people, not “just a job.”
- Park: slide, swing, sand, planting, catching birds.
- School: 4-floor building (grades 1–4), 6-floor building (grades 5–10), bathrooms, sports.

## Rationale

Treat the child’s list as the product spec. Later code should still map to these places and rules, even after a commercial restructure.
