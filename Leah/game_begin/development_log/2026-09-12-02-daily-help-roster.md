# Daily help roster

## Change

帮助站每天换一批人、换一件事。简单的帮忙工钱少，很难的事更累、花更多时间，工钱也更多。

## Rationale

The same four people every day made helping feel like a chore list, not meeting someone new.

## What we refused

- The station still has four people at a time. The pool rotates; it is not an endless crowd.
- Hard jobs cost energy and sometimes food or water. They are not combat.
